The problem

What you said, and what you meant

Speech is fast and messy. Ums, restarts, a list said out loud as one breath. L.O.B.A keeps your words and drops the rest, then pastes the result into the app you're in, written the way that app needs it.

Privacy

There is no server

The speech model and the model that tidies your words both run on your Mac. There's no account, no upload, no subscription. Turn off Wi-Fi and it works the same. Meeting recordings and transcripts are files in a folder you can open, move or delete.

The interface

What it does

Four things, done quietly

Dictate

Talk instead of type

Hold the key anywhere you can type. Let go and the text is pasted, punctuation and lists included.

Polish

Light or Polished

Light keeps your wording and drops the fillers. Polished rewrites what you said into clear sentences, same meaning, nothing added.

Remember

Your words, spelled your way

A dictionary for names and jargon, and a history of the last 50 dictations. Said it in the wrong language? Hear it again in the right one.

Meetings

Record, transcribe, recap

Audio, or audio and video of the call window. You and the others, kept apart. A recap when it ends.

Meetings

The call, written down

Start a meeting and L.O.B.A listens to you and to the call. The others are told apart by voice as Speaker A, B, C; click a name to rename it everywhere. When you stop, a recap appears: summary, decisions, who does what by when. Keep audio only, about 15 MB an hour, or film the call window you pick. Click any line of the transcript to play that moment.

Honest limits

What it will not pretend to do

It doesn't read minds mid-sentence

Switching languages halfway through one sentence can come out in a single language. Short words from another language usually survive.

Two similar voices can become one speaker

Renaming fixes it in a click. One-word replies take the name of whoever spoke just before.

The cleanup needs Ollama

A free app that runs the local model. Without it, L.O.B.A pastes exactly what it heard, which is still fast.

It's a Mac app

Apple Silicon only. No Windows, no iPhone, no web version.

The name

Why L.O.B.A

Loba means speak in Lingala, the language of the Congo river. It also stands for Listen Once, Bring Across: you say it once, and your words arrive where you're typing, in the language you spoke.
